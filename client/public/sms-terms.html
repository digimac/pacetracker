SMS Terms & Consent

This page documents exactly how end users opt in to receive SMS text messages from Sweet Momentum, the exact consent language they see at the point of opt-in, and how to stop receiving messages. No account or login is required to view this page.

Who is sending these messages

Sweet Momentum, a daily performance tracking app at sweetmo.io, operated by Kevin McCarron / Projection Creative LLC, Louisville, KY.

What messages are sent

  • Daily score reminder — sent if a user hasn't logged their score in 3+ days
  • Welcome message — a one-time text sent immediately after a user opts in
  • Momentum Partner alert — sent when a connected accountability partner logs their daily score

All messages are transactional/account-related, not marketing or promotional content.

Message frequency

Varies by user activity and preferences. Typically 0–7 messages per week.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, as determined by the recipient's mobile carrier.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe immediately. Reply HELP to any message for support, or contact track@sweetmo.io. SMS notifications can also be disabled at any time from the Settings page inside the app.

Opt-in method #1 — Account registration

Location: sweetmo.io/#/register, shown immediately after a new user provides a mobile phone number during signup, before any message is sent.

Exact Consent Language Shown at This Screen
Yes, enable SMS notifications
Registration — Step 4 of 4 ("SMS Notifications")

By tapping "Yes, enable SMS notifications" below, you agree to receive recurring transactional text messages from Sweet Momentum (daily score reminders, a one-time welcome message, and momentum partner alerts) at the number provided. Message frequency varies (typically 0–7 msgs/week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Opt-in method #2 — Account settings

Location: sweetmo.io/#/settings, available to any existing user with a saved phone number, at any time.

Exact Consent Language Shown at This Screen
Settings — Profile section, SMS opt-in checkbox

By checking this box, I agree to receive recurring transactional text messages from Sweet Momentum (daily score reminders, a one-time welcome message, and momentum partner alerts) at the number provided. Message frequency varies (typically 0–7/week). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Privacy commitments

Mobile phone numbers collected for this SMS program are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

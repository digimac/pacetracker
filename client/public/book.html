The Framework

Six Daily Metrics That Change Everything

Each chapter of the book explores one metric through real stories — how it's defined, how it's measured, and why it matters more than you think. Score each one daily and watch your momentum compound.

Metric 01
TIME
Did you manage your time with intention today?
Time is the one resource you can't earn back. This chapter explores the relationship between attention, urgency, and the deceptively simple question of whether you spent your hours — or let them be spent.
Metric 02
GOAL
Did you move toward your primary goal today?
Goals without daily motion are just wishes. GOAL tracks the gap between your vision and your action — and challenges you to close it by even one step, every single day.
Metric 03
TEAM
Did you contribute positively to your team today?
No one builds momentum alone. TEAM measures how you show up for the people around you — colleagues, family, partners. The book reveals why this metric is the hidden multiplier of all the others.
Metric 04
TASK
Did you complete your most important task today?
Busyness is not productivity. TASK cuts through the noise and asks the one question that matters: did the right thing get done? This chapter is about ruthless prioritization and the art of finishing.
Metric 05
VIEW
Did you maintain a positive, forward-looking perspective today?
Perspective is the lens through which everything else is filtered. VIEW tracks the quality of your mental horizon — your ability to stay optimistic, curious, and oriented toward possibility even on hard days.
Metric 06
PACE
Did you maintain a sustainable, intentional rhythm today?
Momentum is not a sprint — it's a rhythm. PACE is about sustainable energy management: the discipline to keep moving without burning out, and the wisdom to know the difference between resting and quitting.
